We started with one cold-press machine, one farm in Pune, and one obsession that hasn't moved in 15 years.

How It's Made

From farm
to your hands

Every VIVID bottle follows the same 4-step process. No shortcuts exist at any stage — that's a quality commitment, not a tagline.

01
Source
Hand-picked from 47 partner farms. Only peak-season, grade-A fruit passes our intake standard.
02
Cold Press
Pressed at 4°C with zero heat. Nutrients intact. Flavor maximized. Every enzyme preserved.
03
Quality Test
47 parameters checked — microbial, nutritional, taste. Every batch. No batch ever skipped.
04
Delivered
Cold-chain delivery to your VIVID outlet within 24 hours. Fresh at all times. No exceptions.
